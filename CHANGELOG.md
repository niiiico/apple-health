2026-08-26 - ADR-006: the sink is not the architecture. Sources and derivations become the stable layer; where data lands is a runtime plugin, which retires the ADR-002→005 transport argument without any of them being wrong. Driven by evidence from the Vault: a rendered brief with no coverage boundary produced a "vélo = 0" conclusion that stood wrong for a month (the snapshot ended 07-10; the 48.5 km brick was 07-11), capture thresholds were deleting sub-floor sessions from the record, and the coaching already depends on lap splits and zone durations the pipeline never supplied. Corollaries: Postgres (ingest runs as a pod), coverage recorded as a fact (`ingest_runs.observed_through`, not `max(started_at)`), dated HR zone models (HealthKit has no read-back for historical boundaries), store primitives and derive summaries at query time, analysis runs inside the network with outbound-only egress, Box demoted to a delivery channel, and an internal interaction layer at health.int.dev2.com for what no sensor records. Adds `src/apple_health/store.py` (6 migrations, advisory-locked, tvledger idiom) + psycopg.
